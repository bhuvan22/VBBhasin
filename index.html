Dr. V.B. Bhasin – Senior Orthopaedic Surgeon

With over 43 years of orthopaedic experience, Dr. V.B. Bhasin is known for his precision, surgical expertise, and compassionate care. He is an alumnus of Maulana Azad Medical College and currently practices and Head of the Department of Orthopaedics at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi.

He has received advanced training from Massachusetts General Hospital (USA) and a reputed institute in Germany, enriching his expertise in minimally invasive and arthroscopic surgery.

Special Interests

  • ACL Reconstruction & Arthroscopy
  • Joint Replacement Surgery
  • Sports Injuries

Career Highlights

  • Published research and medical papers
